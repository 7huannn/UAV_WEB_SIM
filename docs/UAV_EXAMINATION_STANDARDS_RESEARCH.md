# UAV examination standards research

Research cutoff/access date: 2026-11-18. This is an engineering source register, not legal advice or an approved examination syllabus. No foreign tolerances are imported into the Vietnamese practice profile. All implemented thresholds are explicitly TRAINING_ONLY. Official-source availability does not establish simulator approval.

## Vietnam - primary target

- Government, **Nghị định 288/2025/NĐ-CP**, effective 2025-11-05: [official signed decree](https://datafiles.chinhphu.vn/cpp/files/vbpq/2025/11/288nd.signed.pdf). Visually inspected articles 3/4 (page 2), 19 (page 16) and 28 (page 25). Distinguishes visual/device-based operation and Hạng A/B licences. Loại 3 is MTOW 2 to <25 kg; Loại 4 starts at 25 kg. The illustrative 15 kg mass suggests Loại 3 only if it represents actual MTOW; the diagram's payload-excluded 10–25 kg range does not establish this. Article 19(3) requires visual-operation licensing from 0.25 kg and describes device-based licensing for the specified ≥2 kg BVLOS/programmed operation. Article 28(2) distinguishes Hạng A theoretical training/testing from Hạng B theoretical plus practical training/assessment. This distinction must be reconciled with authenticated current category-specific examination procedures; do not assume this drawing is a mandatory practical test for every VLOS licence. Binding framework, not simulator approval.
- Ministry of Defence, **Thông tư 146/2025/TT-BQP**, issued/effective 2025-12-25: [government metadata](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?docid=216587&pageid=27160), [signed 20-page circular](https://datafiles.chinhphu.vn/cpp/files/vbpq/2026/01/146-bqp.pdf). Training content and administrative allocation. Visually inspected pages 2–4, signed page 10 (effectiveness), appendix I forms and appendix II equipment. Article 3 defines continuous direct visual observation for VLOS; article 4 includes basic/advanced VLOS, BVLOS, threat management and abnormal situations among training topics. The PDF is scanned; extracting no text is not evidence that it contains no training requirements. Binding framework; task-specific practical sequence, tolerances and category mapping remain UNVERIFIED pending authority confirmation.
- **Quyết định 2426/QĐ-BQP, 2026-05-13**: the Army newspaper reports [basic VLOS examination](https://www.qdnd.vn/xa-hoi/tin-tuc/nhung-diem-liet-can-tranh-khi-sat-hach-thuc-hanh-bay-vlos-1040237) and [GCS/oral assessment](https://www.qdnd.vn/xa-hoi/tin-tuc/noi-dung-sat-hach-ky-nang-tay-dieu-khien-tram-mat-dat-gcs-va-van-dap-tong-hop-1040117). These describe theory/GCS prerequisites and actual VLOS flight with category-specific appendix I. This is an official military publication reporting the decision, **not the signed issuing-authority decision/complete appendix**. Direct page retrieval encountered a redirect loop; indexed text was available. Exact tolerances, penalties, records retention, equipment signatures and applicability are UNVERIFIED; no automatic official pass/fail implemented from press coverage or commercial reproductions.

The supplied diagram is primary **project** evidence: multirotor 10–25 kg excluding payload; 45 × 27 m; H/HA/HB; figure-eight radii; 5 m marker labels; body must not infringe corridor boundaries (diagram note excludes arms/props/gear). It is not accompanied by an authenticated authority/version. A simulator centerline-deviation threshold is not the same as body-envelope contact with V2. Our corridor monitor is explicitly approximate and reviewable.

### Vietnam requirement disposition

- Pilot coordinates and examiner separation: UNVERIFIED; no pilot station explicitly labelled. Configurable provisional station, never H or examiner area by assumption.
- VLOS: supported as fixed-eye training perspective; real unaided visual acuity, line-of-sight judgement and observer/instructor assessment require field validation.
- Preflight, arm, takeoff, hover, orientation, straight flight, full figure eight, perimeter, return, landing, disarm and postflight: implemented as engineering training tasks. Exact Vietnamese prescribed sequence/category and scoring: UNVERIFIED.
- Emergency/abnormal response: practice interruption/abort and instructor event review; no claim to an official emergency syllabus or authentic receiver failsafe.
- Pass/fail: human review required; no official pass available. Authority must confirm flight mode, tolerances, time limits, personnel, records and approved equipment before any official profile is published.

## South Korea - comparison only

Authority: Korea Transportation Safety Authority (TS/KOTSA). [Official pilot examination overview](https://main.kotsa.or.kr/portal/contents.do?menuCode=02020200), undated live page accessed 2026-11-18; cites Aviation Safety Act article 125 and enforcement rule 306. Covers ultralight device qualifications, including unmanned multicopters; qualification subclasses differ. Practical assessment requires satisfactory results for all applicable items; detailed standards are delegated to the national qualification portal. Mandatory within the applicable Korean examination, not a Vietnamese criterion. Implication: preserve individual task results and human judgement, not merely a total score. Exact Korean manoeuvre dimensions/class applicability were not authenticated here and remain UNVERIFIED.

## Japan - comparison only

Authority: MLIT JCAB. [Official licence/standards index](https://www.mlit.go.jp/koku/license.html) lists practical standards applicable from **2026-06-05**. [Second-class multirotor practical detailed rules](https://www.mlit.go.jp/koku/content/001971375.pdf), cover revised 2025-12-05; [first-class rules](https://www.mlit.go.jp/koku/content/001971374.pdf). The second-class source distinguishes preflight oral assessment and practical manoeuvre deductions, examiner decisions, unsafe-flight termination and records. Class-specific and limitation-specific exercises are not interchangeable. Mandatory within that Japanese test. Implication: separate procedural checks, observed metrics, termination and assessor decisions. Do not import its 100-point deduction system or 70-point second-class threshold into Vietnam. Diagram and extension-specific manoeuvres require separate review before a Japanese profile is implemented.

## Australia - comparison only

Authority: CASA. [Annex A to AC 101-01](https://www.casa.gov.au/sites/default/files/2023-07/annex-a-advisory-circular-101-01-remote-pilot-licence-repl-training-course-casa-guidance.pdf), **v6.2, October 2026** (the old-looking URL now serves a newer version). Guidance, explicitly not legislation. Sections 12/12.1.2 distinguish summative RePL practical testing and direct readers to Part 101 MOS Schedule 6 category standards; chapter 10 covers records. Applies to approved RePL training providers/category-specific aircraft. Implication: retain profile version, task evidence and instructor review; no assumption that the Vietnamese course qualifies for RePL credit.

## United Kingdom - comparison only

Authority: UK CAA. [CAP 722B current publication](https://www.caa.co.uk/data-and-publications/publications/documents/content/cap-722b/), **v5.2 / fifth edition amendment 2, 2026-07-16**, [PDF](https://www.caa.co.uk/publication/download/17410), chapter 5 defines FSTD qualification, technical logging, performance/fidelity and instructor-station expectations. Approval policy, not approval of this app. [Current RPC/GVC page](https://www.caa.co.uk/drones/specific-category/remote-pilot-qualifications/level-1-2-and-3-remote-pilot-certificates-and-gvc/) distinguishes qualifications according to operational authorisation; GVC is not the only current pathway. Implication: expose limitations and actual performance, separate instructor and candidate aids, and require qualification evidence before any FSTD credit claim. No GVC/RPC official profile implemented. The [CAA live GVC multirotor assessment guidance](https://regulatorylibrary.caa.co.uk/2019-947/Content/Article%208/GVC%20Fixed%20Wing%20Multirotor.htm) additionally distinguishes mission planning/aircraft preparation, takeoff/abnormal flight, shutdown/inspection/records, and RAE-defined criteria. These are comparison design cues, not transferred Vietnamese scoring rules.

## EU / EASA - comparison only

Authority: European Commission; [Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/947 consolidated 2025-05-01](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2019/947/2025-05-01/eng). Appendix 3 and applicable STS attachments require practical training/continuous assessment, representative conditions and an assessment report identifying pilot, assessor, STS, action marks, overall judgement and feedback. Applies to relevant STS practical assessment, not all open-category certificates; A1/A3 theory and A2 self-practical preparation are distinct pathways. Binding in applicable jurisdictions/categories. Implication: structured evidence, separate task results and assessor conclusions. No STS accreditation or Vietnamese equivalence claimed.

## United States - comparison only

Authority: FAA. [Become a certificated remote pilot](https://www.faa.gov/uas/commercial_operators/become_a_drone_pilot), live page accessed 2026-11-18; [AC 107-2A](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Advisory_Circular/AC_107-2A.pdf), 2021-02-01. Part 107 certification uses the applicable aeronautical knowledge test or eligible Part 61 training pathway, rather than this kind of mandatory manoeuvre flight test. Advisory circular is guidance; underlying Part 107 governs. Implication: label simulator exercises voluntary skill practice, never an FAA Part 107 practical certification test.
