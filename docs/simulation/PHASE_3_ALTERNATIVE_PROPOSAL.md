# Phase 3 controlled alternative — proposed P3-v3, approval required

Prepared 2026-11-19. **This proposal is not implementation authority.** The user approved P3-v1 and the bounded P3-v2 amendment. The P3-v2 *isolated* minimal replacement was built and evaluated before any production sky replacement. It fails the visual conditions for selection. The existing production P3-v1 sky remains in place, and Phase 3 remains incomplete. Phase 4 is unauthorized.

## Why P3-v2 stopped

The [matched diagnostic board](evidence/phase-3/P3_V2_PROTOTYPE_BOARD.html) contains five weather states × five cameras for retained and prototype renders, plus clear/overcast low-sun frames. The prototype runs only from an isolated `/tmp` copy and does not alter production sky code. It uses one local-path source term in a test sky shader and the built-in material fog shader, retains the 5% MOR horizontal coefficient and current weather/sun/snapshot values, and applies the transfer in linear light before the established ACES/sRGB path. The current prototype source is archived with the evidence.

It improves one clear Ground boundary sample but does not meet P3-D or the P3-I art rubric: heavy-weather FPV still has a discrete sky/terrain step; upper clear sky is almost uniform; low sun remains cool instead of developing a credible directional warm-to-blue hierarchy; partly cloudy and overcast scenes preserve repeated horizontal cloud strips; heavy weather loses material/depth hierarchy in a nearly uniform gray veil. A legitimate silhouette or natural horizon gradient was never required to vanish. These defects are visible across the whole composition, so a lower one-dimensional seam metric cannot select the prototype. Two same-fixture prototype profiles meet steady-frame P3-J ratios but show PMREM capture maxima of **657.0 and 134.5 ms**, both failing P3-G's 100 ms stall limit. Moving comparison and performance provenance are recorded in the evidence directory; neither can override the failed visual criterion. There is no human aesthetic acceptance.

The likely mechanisms are specific. The test sky uses a single 220 m optical-distance scale that saturates at low viewing elevation; its sky ray and finite surface ray therefore do not necessarily accumulate the same source term at the geometric horizon. The local source uses fixed molecular/aerosol color weights and a simple direct/ambient blend rather than altitude-dependent solar-path integration, so low-sun spectral color and height-dependent depth are weak. The accepted P1 cloud transport is intact, but the inherited cloud shell's near-horizon sampling/cutoff produces repeated thin strips regardless of sky equation. The context's finite rectangular geometry can still cause hard occlusion; its visible boundary must be distinguished from real silhouettes. These are hypotheses requiring isolated linear-light, depth, ray, and cloud-shell evidence before production selection; this proposal does not authorize tuning by eye without attribution.

## Proposed added scope

P3-v3 would supersede only P3-v2's **minimal local-radiance equation and prototype selection path**. Keep all P3-v1 A–L gates, visual references, art rubric, physics/camera/replay invariants, unchanged visibility presets and the P3-v2 single production sky owner. Authorize a second, still-isolated prototype within the existing Three renderer and atmosphere owner:

1. Derive a bounded, height-resolved, single-scattering daytime model with a molecular layer and a separate near-ground aerosol layer. Document units, scale-height assumptions, phase functions, integration bounds, spectral/color transform, direct-solar attenuation, and the relation of ground-level horizontal contrast to `beta = ln(20)/V`. Use the **same** finite-path transfer/source integral for material aerial perspective, sky rays, cloud eye legs and sun/ambient inputs. No arbitrary color swatch that merely matches the horizon. Numerical approximations are allowed only with error and cost measurements at near/mid/far depths, two eye heights and low sun.
2. Diagnose the existing cloud shell independently of its transport. Repair its near-horizon intersection, mip/LOD or coverage mapping within the one existing cloud layer only if component-isolation frames demonstrate the mechanism. Preserve seed, cloud offset, simulation time, density/current weather values and deterministic motion. Do not introduce volumetric passes, a second cloud system, new noise animation or wall-time movement.
3. Treat terrain/context termination as geometry plus optical depth, not a color-match target. Retain official court and grass geometry. Any change to the noninteractive distant context must have a documented world-space termination and a no-hole proof across five cameras/four azimuths; no invented skyline or camera-height switch.
4. Keep existing direct/hemisphere lights, material albedos/roughness/wetness and exposure until a neutral-patch and lighting-direction diagnostic proves a specific mismatch. Use the same selected environment state and PMREM invalidation policy. If a physically credible model cannot meet the unchanged `V=95/180/260/650 m` weather presets and visual identity together, stop and request a *separate* preset/visibility revision with evidence rather than silently weakening MOR.

This is one replacement candidate, not authorization to retain multiple production skies or accumulate effects. It adds no dependency, full-screen pass, LUT, terrain landmark, global grade, extra fog, camera-specific override, aircraft force, rain renderer or Phase 4 feature. If the second isolated prototype fails, stop again and present evidence; no automatic escalation to volumetrics or another technique.

The principal risks are near-horizon path-length singularities, excessive aerosol dominance at `V=95 m`, implausible low-sun color from an underconstrained source term, cloud shell LOD popping, finite-context geometry exposed by a more honest sky, and six-face PMREM cost. Diagnose each with the isolated component/depth probes before choosing shader detail. A test-only model may use higher-precision numerical integration to establish a reference, but any production approximation must declare its maximum radiance/transmittance error and measured fragment/cube-capture cost. Do not “solve” a real silhouette by forcing terrain and sky RGB to match.

## Prototype selection and validation contract

- Establish a diagnostic matrix with sky-only, finite neutral target, context-only, cloud-only and full-scene views at four azimuths, Ground/Pilot VLOS/Overview/Follow/FPV, clear/partly/overcast/heavy and low sun. Record linear radiance/transmittance and final sRGB with geometric depth/elevation. Exempt documented real silhouettes from smooth-boundary metrics; a renderer discontinuity cannot be exempted by calling it a silhouette.
- Compare matched retained-vs-candidate stills and actual-flight moving segments, including pause/seek/cloud transport and low-sun transition. Use the approved WMO/NASA references for cloud form, illumination, depth and restraint. The P3-I eight-part art rubric must achieve at least 4/5 for each required scene from a named human reviewer; the user's personal aesthetic approval remains mandatory.
- Preserve P3-C's 5% MOR transfer at the prescribed ranges, P3-D's ≤5% unexplained local linear-luminance jump and no >8 px anomalous band, P3-E lighting/material checks, P3-F cloud stability, P3-G PMREM budget and P3-H rain/solar independence. Complete all other P3-A–L gates and the twelve anti-patchwork checks. A pass on one profile or one view is insufficient.
- Repeat comparable 60-warm/300-measured-frame SwiftShader Low 1280×800 DPR1 benchmarks for retained and candidate builds in alternating order, with idle conditions, bundle hashes, per-component isolation, draw calls/triangles, PMREM events and wall/simulation time. Keep P3-J median/p95 ≤1.20× and p99 ≤1.25× accepted P2 values; no silent quality reduction. Report GPU hardware separately as unverified if not tested. Attribute any regression before optimization and prefer fewer shader evaluations or unchanged-scene work over reduced visual fidelity.
- Do not select a production replacement unless the complete scene is more convincing across the matrix, no mandatory gate fails, and the existing model is retired from production. Retain exact prototype sources, fixture metadata, visual board, videos, profile JSON, tests and known artifacts. Then rerun P1–2 regression and P3-A–L on the final build, and present the final board for the user's aesthetic decision. Stop before Phase 4.

**Phase 4 interface if and only if P3-v3 eventually passes:** publish the selected EnvironmentSnapshot time/seed inputs; aerosol/molecular extinction and path-source units; finite eye-leg transfer for surfaces/clouds and future precipitation; one-depth-application/occlusion convention; direct/ambient/PMREM timing and color-space chain; and near/mid/far neutral-target examples. A future rain shader may consume these outputs but cannot add a second atmosphere or compensate for an unaccepted P3 horizon. No precipitation rendering or rain parameter change is included here.

## Decision requested

Approve or revise this **P3-v3 controlled alternative** before any further atmosphere implementation. The present P3-v2 prototype is rejected as a production candidate. Production may retain the P3-v1 partial optical/context changes solely as a preserved incomplete state; it is not a visually accepted Phase 3 result.
