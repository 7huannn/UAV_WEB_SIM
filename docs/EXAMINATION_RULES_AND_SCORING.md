# Examination rules, tasks and scoring

## Status and provenance

Profiles in `src/training/profiles.ts` are v1.0.0; rules are `training-rules-1.0.0`. Every implemented numerical tolerance is **TRAINING_ONLY**, sourced to engineering assumptions rather than a verified Vietnamese scoring provision. The drawing supports a 5 m route label and physical route geometry; using a particular altitude reference and tolerance remains an implementation assumption. No official Vietnamese profile is enabled. The profile viewer exposes every rule value/source and all tasks. Attempts lock profile/rules/aircraft/controller/station/weather/geometry/software metadata and literal reference-path coordinates as copies. Change versions when rules change; never reinterpret old attempts under new tolerances.

## Task definitions

Full Figure Eight and Outer Circuit start with manual climb to 5 m gear altitude, settle for 2 s, then hold over H for 5 s. The former requires H → western entry → both complete lobes in existing direction/order → H; the latter requires H → gate → M1 → M2 → M3 → M4 → M5 → gate → H. Departures/returns are explicit training transfers, not new official paint. Foundations adds four heading holds (0°, +90°, 180°, -90°, 2 s each) and straight H → west entry → H. With the default west pilot station, ±90° include nose/tail toward the pilot. A relocated station needs instructor interpretation of orientation exercises.

Abnormal response practice adds an acknowledged lost-link briefing/recovery-plan step. It does not inject an authentic receiver failure, evaluate spoken judgement automatically or reproduce a verified emergency syllabus. Instructor judgement and an appended assessment are required. Preflight/postflight are self-reported procedural acknowledgements; the simulator cannot inspect real propellers, batteries, airspace permissions or people.

## State machine

NOT_STARTED → PREFLIGHT → READY → ACTIVE → TASK_COMPLETE → LANDING → POSTFLIGHT → FINISHED. Preflight checks gate READY, armed/grounded H and neutral-input/connection conditions gate ACTIVE, observed tasks gate TASK_COMPLETE, a deliberate manual landing phase gates POSTFLIGHT, and all postflight checks gate FINISHED. Landing requires actual low-altitude/low-speed state, disarmed motors and stopped propulsion; landing alone cannot finish tasks. Pause/resume preserves task/gate/dwell state and logs interruption. ABORTED, INCOMPLETE and DISQUALIFIED are terminal training states; invalid transitions return false. DISQUALIFIED means a training safety stop, not an official regulatory decision. Mode restrictions remain active throughout any live attempt.

## Ordered route evaluator

The existing exact arc/tangent/marker route is resampled into ordered gates every 0.5 m, including transfers and return. At most one next gate is credited per fixed step, within 0.65 m horizontal and 0.6 m vertical tolerance. Progress never selects a globally nearest future segment, so the crossover cannot jump to the other lobe. Returning to H cannot substitute for route traversal. Reverse order, skipped loops, incomplete laps and endpoint shortcuts leave unvisited gates. Discontinuous aircraft samples invalidate evidence. Test fixtures that interpolate ideal states are explicitly evaluator unit fixtures; separate tests physically fly both complete courses using synthetic manual velocity inputs through the force model, without assigning aircraft XYZ or invoking reference missions.

Lateral deviation compares the actual center to the next local ordered route segments. This is an approximate corridor monitor; it does not compute exact contact between the rotated fuselage and every digitized V2 neck curve. V1 uses a rotated 0.48 × 0.64 m illustrative fuselage footprint (arms/props/gear excluded), preserving the drawing's body-only distinction. Exact body-envelope/V2 intersection awaits calibrated dimensions and higher-resolution/surveyed boundaries. No claim of official line-infringement scoring.

## Training thresholds and outcomes

Hover: radius 0.6 m, altitude ±0.6 m, speed below 0.35 m/s. Heading exercise tolerance 15°. Route lateral threshold 1 m, altitude threshold ±0.6 m, speed threshold 3 m/s. Safety envelope: V1 body footprint or gear altitude above 12 m or crash terminates the attempt; 600 s training time limit yields incomplete. Start gear altitude must be at most 0.12 m and normalized stick demand within ±0.08. H landing radius 0.6 m; touchdown altitude below 0.08 m, speed below 0.2 m/s, disarmed and motors below 1 rad/s. These are assumptions, including monitor thresholds and sampling tolerances. The configured controller target ceiling and simplified contact crash threshold remain pre-existing airframe/model limits, not legal limits.

Measured metrics: actual 3D trace, speed, altitude/lateral/heading error, gate progress, completed tasks, landing error and seconds over thresholds. Event log records entry/recovery, procedural transitions, pauses, faults and decisions. Heading along a route is a diagnostic metric; it is not silently made a compulsory official nose-direction criterion. Training score starts at 100 and deducts one point per accumulated second per monitored violation, bounded at zero. Multiple simultaneous violations accumulate separately. Score has no automatic pass threshold. A 100-point aborted or incomplete attempt is still incomplete. Completed attempts return HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED; no code path awards an official pass.

Human judgement covers safe situational awareness, actual VLOS orientation recognition, preflight/oral quality, emergency response, outside persons/airspace, realism and competence. Missing hardware/source data remains insufficient evidence. Instructor notes and practice assessments are appended separately, with author and simulation timestamp; they never edit the original samples/result/profile.

## Evidence and integrity

250 Hz physics; recorded state every five steps (50 Hz simulation time), including position, quaternion, velocities/body rates, motor speeds/thrust, normalized/raw input, wind/weather/wetness, task, camera mode/pose/FOV, operating mode and violation state. Simulation time is separate from wall time; dropped catch-up time is recorded. Replay interpolates recorded positions/quaternions without rerunning or altering original flight state; optional recorded-camera playback restores the saved eye/orientation/FOV. JSON carries a SHA-256 checksum of the original payload; CSV preserves sample values and a readable report exposes provenance/limits. Recording is bounded to 600 simulated seconds in all procedural states; imports are limited to 100 MB/30,001 frames. Offline IndexedDB checkpoints every 10 simulated seconds and final snapshots survive reload. A non-final checkpoint is visibly incomplete; it cannot be resumed into a fabricated completed result. Finalized records cannot be overwritten through the storage API. Checksums detect changed content but provide no authenticated device signature, proctor security or certified records-retention guarantee. Export a backup; browser storage may be cleared or quota-limited.
