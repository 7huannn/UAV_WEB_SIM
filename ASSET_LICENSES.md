# Assets and licenses

All downloads are local at runtime. No remote scripts, CDN or remote imagery are required to fly.

- **Concrete Floor 01**: Poly Haven, https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_01 — CC0 1.0. `public/assets/textures/concrete-{color,normal,roughness}.jpg` are the original 2K diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps. Metadata: https://api.polyhaven.com/files/concrete_floor_01.
- **Leafy Grass**: Poly Haven, https://polyhaven.com/a/leafy_grass — CC0 1.0. `public/assets/textures/grass-{color,normal,roughness}.jpg`, original 2K maps. Metadata: https://api.polyhaven.com/files/leafy_grass.
- Asset licensing: https://polyhaven.com/license (CC0). Download script: `scripts/download-assets.py`.
- Arena diagram: provided by the project owner; retained solely as project reference. No assertion of public-domain licensing. Original JPEG and rotated lossless PNG under `assets/reference/`.
- UAV airframe and background grass blade geometry: authored for this project. GLB generated by `scripts/export-drone.ts`; materials, sky/cloud shaders and painted geometry are project source.
- Three.js and its bundled addons: MIT; React: MIT. Dependency licenses remain in their npm packages.

The concrete/grass textures are general outdoor assets, not photographs of the actual competition venue.

- **Grass Medium 01**: Rico Cilliers (modeling), Rob Tuytel (photography), Poly Haven, https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_medium_01 — CC0 1.0. Original 1K glTF, binary geometry, diffuse/OpenGL-normal/ARM maps and alpha mask are stored under `public/assets/models/grass-medium-01/`. Three selected small/medium tuft variants are instanced in deterministic world-space tiles; the full original glTF and all its local dependencies are retained so regeneration is reproducible. Metadata: https://api.polyhaven.com/files/grass_medium_01.

- Refinement assets: no additional external downloads. The filtered cloud-noise atlas, tile distribution, middle-distance blade clusters, concrete bevel and context geometry are authored in project source. The optional visual context matte reuses the already licensed Leafy Grass maps.
